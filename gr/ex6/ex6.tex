\section{Warmpup}
Starting from the Einstein equation
\begin{equation}
    R_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2}Rg_{\mu\nu} = T_{\mu\nu}
\end{equation}
Taking the trace
\begin{equation}
    R - \frac{1}{2}Rg = T
\end{equation}
Solving for $R$:
\begin{equation}
    R = \frac{2}{1-g}T
\end{equation}
Assigning this back into the original Einstein's equation and solving for $R_{\mu\nu}$ we get
\begin{equation}
    R_{\mu\nu} = T_{\mu\nu} + \frac{g_{\mu\nu}}{1 - g}T
\end{equation}
